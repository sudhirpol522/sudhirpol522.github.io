f:T4a5,<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Implemented a tiled, non-causal FlashAttention-1 forward kernel in CUDA with online softmax, using one thread block per (batch, head, query tile) and shared-memory staging for the Q, K, V, score, and output-accumulator tiles. Both kernel variants are exposed to PyTorch as a C++/CUDA extension via pybind11.</p>
<h2>Selected results</h2>
<ul>
<li>Traced the first implementation's shared-memory bank conflicts in the QK<sup>T</sup> inner loop and removed them by padding the K tile to (d+1).</li>
<li>Replaced a lane-0 serialized softmax reduction with warp-shuffle (<code>__shfl_xor_sync</code>) max and sum reductions across all 32 lanes.</li>
<li>Built a correctness-gated benchmark harness that validates both kernels against <code>torch.nn.functional.scaled_dot_product_attention</code> to a max absolute error under 5e-2 (FP32) before timing, sweeping sequence lengths 128 to 1024 at batch 8, 12 heads, head dimension 64.</li>
</ul>
