f:T63d,<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>In small-vLLM, I built the pieces that let an inference engine serve multiple requests while working within a GPU memory budget. It uses a paged KV cache with reference-counted blocks to reuse shared prefixes, continuous batching to schedule requests, and chunked prefill and preemption to manage the work.</p>
<p>I connected those pieces to FlashAttention, CUDA Graphs, GPTQ and AWQ weight loading, and a streaming API. I also benchmarked the engine against vLLM on an RTX A6000; the measurements and workload settings are below.</p>
<h2>Selected results</h2>
<ul>
<li>Integrated FlashAttention and a PyTorch reference attention backend, with CUDA Graphs for decode execution.</li>
<li>Implemented paged KV caching with reference-counted blocks for prefix reuse, continuous batching, chunked prefill, and request preemption.</li>
<li>Benchmarked steady-state generation on one RTX A6000 with a 4,096-token context limit, one request at a time, and 512 generated tokens per request. Measured 189.8 tokens/s on Qwen3-0.6B and 36.5 tokens/s on Qwen3-8B, compared with 393.9 and 42.4 tokens/s for vLLM, respectively, under the same benchmark conditions.</li>
<li>Integrated GPTQ and AWQ INT4 weight loading, handling their distinct packed checkpoint layouts and routing execution through a shared Marlin backend.</li>
<li>Exposed OpenAI-compatible completion and chat endpoints with streaming, alongside model discovery, health, and metrics endpoints in FastAPI.</li>
</ul>
