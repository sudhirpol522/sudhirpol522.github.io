NFL Big Data Bowl

Probabilistic play outcome modeling with calibrated predictions and football specific features.

Overview

This project frames yard gain prediction as a probabilistic problem and predicts the complete cumulative distribution of outcomes rather than a single point estimate.

Selected results

  • Engineered play level spatial and football specific features from tracking data.
  • Compared logistic regression, SVM, random forest, gradient boosted trees, and XGBoost.
  • Calibrated output probabilities with isotonic regression.
  • Achieved a CRPS of 0.017 and ranked 237th in the competition.
